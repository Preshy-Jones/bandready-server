# IELTS Question Sources (Writing + Speaking)

This project uses a curated bank of IELTS-style and past-paper style prompts compiled from public IELTS preparation and official sample resources.

## Primary Sources

- IELTS official sample test materials (Writing Task 1/Task 2 prompts):
  - https://www.ielts.org/for-test-takers/sample-test-questions
  - https://www.ielts.org/for-teachers/ielts-for-teachers
- British Council IELTS preparation pages (Speaking topic structures and sample questions):
  - https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare/free-ielts-english-practice-tests/speaking
  - https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/take-ielts/prepare/free-ielts-english-practice-tests/writing
- IDP IELTS preparation pages (Speaking and Writing question formats and recurring topics):
  - https://ielts.idp.com/prepare/article-ielts-writing-task-2-topics-and-questions
  - https://ielts.idp.com/prepare/article-ielts-speaking-part-2-topics-and-questions
- Public speaking topic collections used by candidates to track recent/recurring IELTS topics:
  - https://ieltsmaterial.com/list-of-ielts-speaking-part-1-topics/
  - https://ieltsmaterial.com/ielts-cue-card-topics/

## Coverage Notes

- Writing Task 1 prompts in `task1-questions.ts` include a mix of official sample-style Academic visuals and General Training letter prompts aligned to IELTS task format.
- Writing Task 2 prompts in `task2-questions.ts` span common IELTS question types:
  `opinion`, `discussion`, `problem_solution`, `advantages_disadvantages`, and `two_part`.
- Speaking prompts in `prisma/seed.ts` are distributed across Parts 1, 2, and 3, with additional concentration on:
  `Accommodation`, `Achievement`, `Art`, `Books`, `Childhood`, `Culture`, `Education`, `Experience`.
